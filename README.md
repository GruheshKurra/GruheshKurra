I'm an MSc student at Imperial, working on **large language models and natural language processing**. I look at how models represent words and meaning, how one prompt can give several different answers, and how to make models smaller without losing quality.

Before Imperial I studied Computer Science and Engineering at KL University, Hyderabad (CGPA 9.72 / 10). I was a research intern at IIIT Hyderabad, and I also built integration software for enterprise clients at InfoAjax.
